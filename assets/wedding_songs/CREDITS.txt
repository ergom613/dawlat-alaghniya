YouTube (default, first in the playlist)
---------------------------------------
دبكه ودبيكه — Asala Yousef
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2Ztwby_SXGM

This song is copyrighted. It is NOT downloaded, ripped, or rehosted.
The preview site streams it with the official YouTube IFrame Player API
after the visitor taps 🔊. Volume is set with player.setVolume() from
the camera's distance to the square (0 when far away). A small official
player stays visible while this track is playing (YouTube's rules).

The five Pixabay instrumentals below stay in the playlist after the YouTube track.

These five tracks are the default songs for the preview wedding square
(ساحة عرس). They are instrumentals released under the Pixabay Content
License, which allows use in commercial and non-commercial projects
without mandatory attribution (attribution is appreciated). You may not

IMPORTANT
---------
- These are royalty-free "dabke fusion" / festive Middle Eastern
  instrumentals, NOT famous commercial Syrian dabke hits (those have
  copyright and must not be used).
- Pixabay marked all five as "AI generated".
- Pixabay does not guarantee that a track will never be claimed by
  Content ID on TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, or YouTube. For site
  playback on the map this is usually fine; if a daily video uses the
  same bed and gets muted, switch tracks or use a file Mohammad provides.
- Traditional village dabke recordings with a clear reuse license are
  very rare. These were the closest clearly licensed dabke-sounding
  tracks that could be obtained.

Rejected / not used
-------------------
- Commercial hits (Omar Alabdallat, Toni Qattan, etc.): copyrighted.
- FMA "Darbuka Groove" (Serge Quadrado): CC-BY-NC — non-commercial only.
- Freesound live-wedding field recordings: likely contain copyrighted songs.
- Mixkit tracks under an "Arabic" tag (e.g. Feast From The East, Lazy Camel):
  licensed, but not dabke / not Syrian wedding dance music.
- Atlas • Syria (Vadim_Makes_Sound): Pixabay Content License, but cinematic
  / dreamy qanun travel bed, not a dance dabke cue.
- Sharid (EarsForMars): tagged Morocco / desert / Egypt more than Levantine dabke.
