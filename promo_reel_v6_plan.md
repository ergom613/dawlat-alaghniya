## Known nits

- «بيبلّش خيمة» and «اليخت بينفتح» are a bit mumbled in the VO; typography carries them.
- The live L15 yacht is too small for a satisfying close-up; the L30 wide shot is the clear yacht reveal.
- «يتبع» is spoken at 27.20 while the on-screen word is still «تابعنا»; «يتبع..» appears on the end card at 28.30.
- End-card music is fairly quiet (the bed is looped; VO has already finished).
- Tiny OSM credit at the bottom of the end card, no URL.
